Challenges, city discovery and tastings

On Your Marks, Get Set, Explore!

In teams of 4 to 8 people (or more), set off on a captivating adventure in the heart of Brussels! Armed with a roadbook, solve an intriguing mystery, take on challenges and above all… blind-taste small glasses of beer in three iconic bars. But be warned: if you want to win, you’ll have to guess the flavours and fill in your tasting sheet. Part friendly get-together, part competition: who will come out on top?

Fun, Lively Hosting

Our host welcomes you and is on hand throughout the adventure, right up to the reveal of the answers and the overall winner. Ready to take on the challenge?

Challenges for Everyone!

On the programme: riddles, photo challenges, observation quizzes and, of course, tastings! The perfect game for beer lovers, the curious and adventurers looking for an original way to (re)discover Brussels.

An Experience Open to All

Not a beer fan? No problem, choose a soft drink and enjoy the experience! A unique, festive activity, perfect with friends, family or as a couple.
