What to do on a stopover in Brussels?

23 February 2021, by Beer My Guest

Brussels: the very embodiment of beer around the world

World-famous for its age-old brewing scene and its architectural riches, Brussels is a city L’Échappée Bière simply couldn’t miss out on.

So it goes without saying that we recommend this beer-themed tour of the city: a chance to appreciate Brussels culture even more by combining sightseeing with pleasure.

Our group activities are suited to businesses (seminars, team building) and private groups (stag and hen parties) alike.
The Treasure Hunt is a storyline we created ourselves, in which the aim is to gather clues hidden all over the city that will help you solve a riddle.

A fictional story leads you wandering through the cobbled lanes surrounding the famous Grand Place. Once there, you can admire the Gothic style of this iconic square, in the tradition of the Flemish cities. The Grand Place is beautiful by day and by night, when its illuminations reveal another side to its façades.

This activity introduces you to the history of lambic, the quintessential Brussels beer. In total you’ll make 4 stops in must-visit beer bars to learn the art of tasting.

The L’Échappée Bière treasure hunt promises a convivial time while learning about the history of the streets and monuments of Brussels.

If you want to take the beer experience further, the tasting activity is made for you. We’ll talk brewing in a beer-science workshop, and an introduction to tasting will teach you everything (or almost everything) about beer.

Brussels and comics: a love story to share

Even though we could talk about it for hours, Brussels isn’t just about beer. There are plenty of reasons to visit, and among them are some rather more unusual activities…

If you’re heading to Brussels, you should know it is also the world capital of comics. With so many murals adorning walls all over the city, you can’t miss them! There’s even a guided route that brings these works to life, featuring famous characters such as Lucky Luke, Asterix and Obelix, Gaston Lagaffe and Boule et Bill.

And if you happen to be a big comics fan, you can go all the way and visit the Comics Museum for a total immersion in this world.

Staying with art, we’d like to tell you about a recent museum of international standing that caught our eye: the MIMA, Millennium Iconoclast Museum of Art, dedicated to contemporary creation, including street art. The museum is housed in the imposing buildings of the former Belle-Vue brewery, along the canal in the centre of Brussels. Places to eat and panoramic views over the canal await you there.

See the city the easy way, by bike

Hopping on a bike is a practical way to soak up all the different atmospheres the capital has to offer and admire every corner while getting a breath of fresh air. Ride through the historic centre with its rich heritage and reach the neighbouring districts via green spaces to appreciate every facet of Brussels.

Maps are available to guide you along the various routes. You can also cycle along the canal and hop aboard the Waterbus, another form of public transport, for a trip through the city’s industrial past.

A ride through the Sonian Forest, the city’s true green lung, is a great way to escape to somewhere fresh and open.

More information on routes and bike hire can be found on the Brussels tourist office website.

Tours led by locals: the Greeters

Ideal for group activities, it was in the end the walks with a local guide that really caught our eye. Born from participatory tourism, this initiative is a genuine source of multicultural exchange and mutual enrichment, which we wholeheartedly support and recommend. These tours are led by Greeters, volunteer guides who give their time to share their favourite places and anecdotes during a bike outing. As you’ve probably gathered, this activity is free, and we want to highlight how wonderfully friendly it is. Someone who loves showing you around their neighbourhood, chatting with you and answering your questions: it’s inviting and it’s warm. The tour calendar can be found on the Brussels Greeteers website. Tours can cover different themes, which you can find on the booking platform.

Let’s be clear: you can’t see all of Brussels in a day… The city is packed with must-see places and all kinds of tourist activities. The suggested programme will let you focus on the essentials in a single day, whether for a seminar or a sightseeing visit.

World-renowned for its beers, its comics, its UNESCO World Heritage monuments and its warm culture, Brussels has countless assets that make it a destination of choice.

Has this first glimpse of the city already whetted your appetite? Then keep following us to discover other, more unusual sides of this city very soon.
