Top 5 beer bars in Brussels

23 February 2021, by Beer My Guest

Moeder Lambic, Place Fontainas and Saint-Gilles

Moeder Lambic is the benchmark among Brussels beer bars for purists! Here, beer is a religion and the selection is uncompromising. More than fifty beers are available on draught. Their permanent list features the very best of Belgian beers, while their rotating list mainly showcases beers from all over the world. Venture in and you’re guaranteed unexpected, top-quality discoveries! As the name suggests, you’ll also find a fine selection of lambics, the traditional spontaneously fermented beer of Brussels. Moeder is also a great place to spend time, thanks to its warm décor and a choice of high-end cured meats and cheeses to snack on. Both venues, Saint-Gilles and Place Fontainas, are well worth a visit for a unique tasting experience! In fact, you’ll pass through the latter if you take part in one of our treasure hunts in Brussels…

Nüetnigenough, 25 rue du Lombard

Looking for a simple, cosy, traditional atmosphere? You’ve come to the right place! Here, everything is done to make you feel at home. This spot is truly made for food lovers and fans of good beer. Once inside, you’ll easily see why… The menu is mainly made up of traditional Belgian/Brussels dishes (stoemp, ham hock, veal meatballs, chicken fricassee, etc.) served in very generous portions. As for the beer list, it will get your mouth watering with its magnificent selection of beers arranged by brewery. Among others, you’ll find beers from the excellent Brasserie de la Senne and De Dochter van de Korenaar! A must during any stay in Brussels. It can sometimes be hard to get a table, the place is so popular: first come, first served! Don’t worry, you can wait with a beer at the bar until a table frees up!

Nüetnigenough is just a stone’s throw from the Royal Galleries of Saint-Hubert.

L’Amère à boire, 8 rue du Belvédère, Place Flagey

A must-visit haunt for discerning beer lovers, L’Amère à boire offers a selection of more than 150 specialty beers, some of them displayed on the bar’s walls. This carefully curated selection deliberately steers clear of the classics to showcase original beers from small breweries. The bar also stands out for its original events, such as the “Apéros dessinatoires”, held once a month on random dates, which aim to inspire collaborative drawings. Another event, “Le Pain dans ta gueule”, takes place every Wednesday so that everyone can get hold of organic, local, naturally leavened sourdough bread. Trendy and original, L’Amère à boire is sure to win you over with its style. Note that it can sometimes be hard to find a seat at the end of the week.

See the Facebook page.

Gist, 30 Place de la Vieille Halle aux Blés

One of the latest arrivals on the new Brussels beer bar scene, Gist opened its doors in 2017. Jenlain and Jodie, two friendly beer enthusiasts, are at the helm. After gaining experience, notably at the famous Moeder, they launched their own business. With a 100% craft selection and no fewer than 18 beers on tap, this is a place for lovers of good products! It’s simple, warm (the wooden décor works its magic) and unpretentious. Gist guarantees an authentic atmosphere, with two turntables whose sound makes the tasting even more enjoyable… A bar that will keep getting people talking and is set to become a must!

See the Facebook page.

Daringman, 37 rue de Flandre

Welcome to this typically Brussels den. Daringman is one of the “bruin cafés”, or “brown cafés”: places with a traditional look and wood-panelled interiors. These typical bars usually make you feel as if you were sitting in an old-fashioned living room. Sadly, traditional brown cafés are tending to disappear from the capital in favour of newer, more modern venues. This intimate bar, with 40 seats, is wonderfully warm and… timeless. Here, everyone is welcome, and the landlady makes it her mission. With its small concert room upstairs, it has a very special atmosphere that is hard to resist. The beer selection is simple but effective. Among others, you’ll find all the Belgian Trappists as well as Gageleer, an organic specialty beer served in only a handful of places! A place not to be missed! And one of the best bars in the world according to several media outlets…
