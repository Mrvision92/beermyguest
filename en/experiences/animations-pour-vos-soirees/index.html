Evening Entertainment

It’s often the little touches that make a great evening! Cocktail party, dinner, incentive or corporate event: choose one or more entertainment options to surprise your guests.

Mentalist & Magician

Close-up magic and mentalism, right among your guests.

Tintin & Captain Haddock (actors)

The quintessential Belgian touch: iconic characters brought to life by actors who mingle and interact with your guests.

Singers & Saxophone

An elegant, festive musical atmosphere, with the option of adding a distinctly Belgian touch.

Beer Casino

Chips, blind tastings and challenges around our casino tables.

Elegant Beer Bar

A genuine, stylish pop-up beer bar offering a selection of exceptional Belgian craft beers, carefully chosen and far removed from the usual names. A premium experience, served and presented by our experts.

Beer & Chocolate Bar

A gourmet stand where your guests discover and taste beers and chocolates at their own pace.

Chocolate Workshop

An interactive stand where everyone can make their own chocolate, or a sweet activity served up at dessert time.

A fun, convivial activity and a personalised souvenir to take home.

Artists, performances and a real show to take your evening to another level.

Photobooth

An activity open throughout the evening, with instant souvenirs for your guests.
