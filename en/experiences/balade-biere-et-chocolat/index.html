Beer and Chocolate Walk

Beer & Chocolate Walk – A delicious way to discover Brussels

In Brussels – and also in Ghent or Bruges – Beer My Guest invites you to discover Belgian gastronomy in a different way, through tasty, local and convivial walks.

Set off to explore Brussels through its charming little streets, its must-see landmarks such as the Grand Place and Manneken Pis, and above all through its most iconic flavours!

On the menu: craft beers and fine chocolates, tasted at renowned chocolatiers and in a characterful traditional estaminet. A convivial walk where you get a taste of the local culture as much as its gourmet specialities.

It’s a hit every time: a fun, warm and tasty experience that delights every audience.

The standard walk lasts between 2h and 2h30, but can be shortened or extended to suit you.

And for true foodies, other typically Belgian treats can be added too: speculoos, shrimp croquettes…

A little tip: don’t come planning to stick to a diet!
