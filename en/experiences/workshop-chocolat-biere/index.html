Chocolate & Beer Workshop

Beer Quiz & Chocolate Workshop: the best of Belgium in one activity!

A two-in-one activity that’s original, indulgent and deliciously Belgian! Your group is split into two teams that take turns at two iconic experiences.

Beer Quiz

Put your senses and knowledge to the test with 4 Belgian beer tastings!

Opaque glasses, surprising questions and little challenges add extra spice to the experience.

Colour, aromas, alcohol content… are you really sure of your answers? A friendly challenge where brewing culture, fun and team spirit come together.

Chocolate Workshop

Time to get creative! Alongside a Brussels master chocolatier, discover the secrets of Belgian chocolate and make your own creations to enjoy on the spot or take home.

An indulgent, hands-on and 100% Belgian moment.

The activity can be held in the centre of Brussels, at another suitable venue or directly at your conference or event venue. The format is fully flexible to suit your group, your budget and your logistical requirements.

Duration: 2 to 3 hours

Groups: from 2 to 180 participants

Location & format: tailor-made

Fancy bringing together beer, chocolate and team spirit? Ask for a quote and let’s create the ideal format for your group together!

  • Capacity: 2 to 300 people
