Beer Casino

A blind discovery:

At each of our 2 beer casino tables, our beer-expert croupiers hand out chips to participants and serve them a small glass of beer, blind. Using their palate and the host’s clues, players place bets on various elements: the colour, flavours and aromas, origin and alcohol content of each beer will all be put to the test. Only exceptional Belgian beers feature in this activity: lambics, abbey beers, IPAs, double IPAs, beers aged in whisky or cognac casks, sour ales, German Rauchbier…

The goal is clear:

to help you discover beer from every angle, in a playful way! Prizes await the most perceptive players! An individual or team challenge.

At a glance

  • Capacity: 2 to 300 people
  • Duration: 1.5h - to be agreed
  • Languages: FR/NL/EN/ES
  • Location: Brussels, anywhere in Belgium
  • Time together with colleagues
  • Team bonding
