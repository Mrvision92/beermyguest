Hidden Brussels & Belgian Beer World

What if you uncovered what lies beneath the streets of Brussels before taking in the view from one of its finest rooftops? Follow the traces of a vanished Brussels: the old river now buried beneath the city, neighbourhoods transformed, the epidemics that shook the capital and the stories that shaped its historic centre. Your route leads you to the majestic, fully renovated Brussels Stock Exchange, where the experience continues at the Belgian

Beer World to discover another essential part of Belgian heritage: its brewing culture.

History, traditions, brewing techniques and anecdotes: you don’t need to be a beer lover to enjoy the visit. Here, beer is discovered first and foremost as a genuine part of Belgium’s cultural heritage.

And to round things off in style: head up to the Stock Exchange rooftop, with its exceptional view over Brussels and the chance to enjoy a beer or an alcohol-free alternative.

Duration: 1h to 2h

Groups: 2 to 300 participants

Location: Brussels Stock Exchange, historic centre

Brussels beneath your feet, its history all around you and its panorama from the rooftops: request your quote and discover the capital in a whole new way!
