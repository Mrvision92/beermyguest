Chocolate and beer treasure hunt (or just chocolate

A Fun Foodie Treasure Hunt for Colleagues!

Looking for an original activity in Brussels for your company, your team or your group of colleagues?

Treat them to a unique team building experience that combines a treasure hunt, cultural discovery and culinary delights!

Discover Brussels from a different angle with a lively, interactive treasure hunt. The starting point is the Café des Halles, right in the heart of Brussels. There, an enthusiastic host will welcome you and explain the rules of the game. Then, roadbook in hand, set off in teams to solve an urban mystery packed with fun challenges: riddles, observation quizzes, photo challenges and more!

Tasty stops that make all the difference

All along the route, enjoy tastings at three outstanding chocolatiers and sample local beers in two traditional Brussels bars.

What if I don’t drink? No problem!

The activity is perfectly suited to people who don’t drink alcohol or who have food intolerances: alternatives are always provided so that everyone can fully enjoy the experience.

Team spirit… right to the very end!

At the end of the game, meet up with the host for the marking of the challenges, the tallying of the points and the prize-giving for the winning team in a friendly atmosphere.

Why choose this treasure hunt?

  • An ideal activity for your company seminars, afterwork drinks or team building events in Brussels
  • Perfect for strengthening team cohesion, encouraging interaction and discovering the city from a different angle
  • An experience accessible to everyone, suited to all dietary requirements and fitness levels. Book an unusual tailor-made or standard activity for your team in Brussels!
