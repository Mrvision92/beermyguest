Beer Workshop in the Basilica

Basilica, Panorama & Beers

An exceptional monument, a spectacular view and a tasting your guests won’t forget in a hurry.

Step inside the impressive National Basilica of Koekelberg, an Art Deco gem and one of the most remarkable monuments in Brussels. With your guide, discover its architecture, its history and its monumental spaces before heading up high to take in an incredible 360° panorama over Brussels.

Then it’s time for our “Beer and the Church” experience: a tasting of abbey and Trappist beers that traces the fascinating links between monastic traditions, history and Belgian brewing know-how.

A cultural and sensory experience in a truly extraordinary setting, which can be adapted to different formats depending on the size and expectations of your group.

Duration: 2 to 2.5 hours

Groups: from 1 to 120 participants, or even more depending on the format

Location: National Basilica of Koekelberg, Brussels

Format: visit, panorama & beer experience, adaptable to your group

Looking for another exceptional venue?

We can also create experiences and tastings in other iconic or unusual locations: at the Atomium, in the ruins of the former palace of Brussels, in an old guildhall still dedicated to beer, in little-known breweries… and many more possibilities.

Looking for a venue that’s truly out of the ordinary? Tell us about your group and what you have in mind: we’ll create your tailor-made experience.
