Gourmet Walk – Food Tour

A must and one of our best-sellers for seeing the city from a different angle! In Brussels, and also available in Bruges, Ghent & Antwerp.

Explore the city with a passionate local guide, discover its must-see sights and its quieter backstreets, hear the stories behind them and, above all, taste the very best of Belgium. Our Food Tour brings together sightseeing, chocolate, beer and good company in a single experience.

Brussels and its pralines

As you stroll through the historic centre, your guide reveals the iconic sights and hidden secrets of Brussels. Along the way, you’ll stop at two chocolatiers to taste three carefully selected handmade pralines.

The beer stop

Next, it’s off to a traditional Brussels bar to taste two Belgian beers in tasting-size servings. Served blind, they’re a chance to play, compare, chat and discover the richness of our brewing culture.

A cultural, delicious and interactive experience – the perfect way to discover the city without ever feeling like you’re on a traditional guided tour.

Hungry for more?

Plenty of extras can be added on request: a warm waffle, traditional fries, cuberdons, hot chocolate… and during the festive season, why not add a mulled wine and turn the tour into a true Christmas experience?

Extra tastings, other Belgian specialities, a shorter or longer route:

everything can be tailored to your group, the season and your schedule.

Duration: ideally 2 to 2.5 hours, adaptable on request

Groups: from 2 to 400 participants

Location: Brussels – historic centre. Also available in Bruges, Ghent and Antwerp

Format: guided walking tour & food tastings, fully adaptable

A true best-seller for tasting the city as much as discovering it. Ask for your tailor-made quote!
