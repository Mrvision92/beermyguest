Beer Casino

Place your bets… on the right beer!

At our Beer Casino tables, our beer-expert croupiers hand out the chips and serve Belgian beers blind. Then it’s up to you to bet on the right answer: colour, aromas, flavours, origin, alcohol content… Trust your palate! A fun and original way to discover Belgian beer, with just the right amount of competition to challenge your colleagues.

Two signature formats

Tasting & Challenge

A genuine group activity lasting 1 to 1.5 hours, for up to 30 participants. Everyone plays and tastes together around our tables.

Beer Casino Stand

Ideal for an incentive evening, a cocktail party, a reception or a corporate event. Guests join the tables as they please, play a few rounds and carry on with their evening. The activity can run for 2 or 3 hours, or even accompany your event for a whole day.

Duration: from 1 hour to a full day, depending on the format

Groups: from 1 to 30 participants for the tasting format – much larger capacity for the stand format

Location: in a space reserved for your group, in Brussels or anywhere else in Belgium

Format: group tasting or continuous entertainment during your event

Chips, blind tasting and Belgian beers: your move! Ask for a quote and choose your format
