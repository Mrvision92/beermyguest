Foodie treasure hunt

Discover Brussels as you’ve never seen it before!

Set off on an adventure in the heart of Brussels with our interactive treasure hunts! In teams, follow the clues, take on challenges and discover the city from a different angle, with quirky anecdotes, culture and plenty of good humour.

Our routes lead you through narrow lanes and to the hidden curiosities of the historic centre, while also taking in must-sees such as the Grand Place and Manneken Pis. The perfect balance between off-the-beaten-track Brussels and its iconic landmarks.

Several experiences are available:

  • Comic Strip, Street Art & Chocolate treasure hunt
  • Beer & Chocolate treasure hunt
  • Tailor-made treasure hunts, adapted to your group and your wishes. Ideal for companies, team buildings, groups of friends or private events, each treasure hunt combines discovery, fun and team spirit for an original experience in the heart of Brussels.

Duration: 2 to 2.5 hours

Groups: from 2 to 300 participants

Ready for the adventure? Ask for a quote and find the ideal format for your group!
