Beer & chocolate pairing

Beer & Chocolate Pairing – Two Belgian icons, one experience 🍺🍫

What if two of Belgium’s greatest symbols also made the perfect match?

Led by a lively professional zythologist, discover the surprising pairings between Belgian beers and handmade chocolates. On the menu: 4 carefully selected beers and 6 quality pralines to taste and pair, to understand how aromas, textures and flavours can complement each other… or sometimes surprise you!

An indulgent, interactive and thoroughly Belgian experience that blends tasting, anecdotes, culture and good company.

Not a beer drinker? No problem: alcohol-free alternatives can be provided on request so that everyone can fully enjoy the experience.

Duration: 1 to 1.5 hours

Groups: from 2 to 300 participants, or even more on request

Location & format: at one of our partner venues in the heart of Brussels’ historic centre, at another venue selected especially for your group, or directly at your conference or event venue, anywhere in Belgium

Want to surprise your group with two Belgian classics in a single experience?

Ask for a quote and let’s create your Beer & Chocolate Pairing together!
