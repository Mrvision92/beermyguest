Historic and modern brewery tour

Historic Brewery & Modern Beers 🍺

From beer brewed the old-fashioned way to today’s creations: discover Brussels through its brewing history.

The experience begins in a truly unique historic brewery, a stone’s throw from the Gare du Midi. A real Brussels gem where beer is still produced using ancestral methods, far removed from industrial brewing. Here you’ll discover lambic, the authentic pre-industrial historic beer of Brussels, made by spontaneous fermentation. If you’ve never tasted it, be prepared: its aromas and flavour are like virtually no modern beer. Surprise guaranteed!

Then continue on foot towards the historic centre with your guide, who will share anecdotes, stories and Brussels curiosities along the way.

And to finish, a change of era! Head to a Brussels bar to discover contemporary beers through a blind tasting of three beers and an interactive quiz.

Want to push the contrast even further?

Depending on timing and availability, a visit to a modern brewery can also be added. You’ll then truly discover both faces of Brussels beer: the methods of yesteryear versus contemporary brewing.

Duration: 1 to 3 hours, adaptable depending on options

Groups: from 2 to 100 participants

Location: Brussels, starting near the Gare du Midi and heading towards the historic centre
