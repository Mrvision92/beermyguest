Beer Day in Brussels

A perfect day: authenticity, beer, discovery and fun guaranteed! 🍻

Enjoy a Unique Day in Brussels!

Dive into the heart of the capital and its rich brewing culture in three unforgettable stages! An authentic, tasty and playful experience to discover Brussels differently.

A Delicious Lunch in a Traditional Restaurant

After this brewing immersion, it’s time for a delicious meal in a restaurant much loved by locals. Enjoy a dish of your choice, with a dessert, two drinks and a coffee/tea.

Immersive Tour of the Historic Brewery

Start the day by exploring the oldest brewery in Brussels! A preserved gem that keeps alive the art of lambic and gueuze, the city’s iconic beers, which survived the industrial era. On a fascinating guided tour, delve into the world of malt, hops and yeast, then taste three beers from their range.

Beer treasure hunt

To round off the day in style, set off on a playful adventure through Brussels! Explore its secret backstreets and discover its must-see monuments.
