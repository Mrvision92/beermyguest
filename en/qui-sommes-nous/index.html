Who are we?

Your local partner in Brussels and Belgium

Beer My Guest is a local receptive agency, active since 2015 in Brussels and across Belgium.

What do we do? We help you discover Belgium the way we love it: from the inside.

Guided tours, food experiences, team buildings, incentives, events, unusual venues, cycling, chocolate, beer, culture, heritage… We design experiences for small and large groups in Brussels, as well as in Antwerp, Ghent, Bruges and elsewhere in Belgium.

Our greatest strength is being 100% local. We know the best addresses, the hidden spots, the partners we love working with and the experiences that are truly worth it.

Our goal? To help you avoid the tourist traps and experience an authentic, friendly Belgium full of surprises.

Magali De Vinck – Founder & Brussels ambassador

A proud Brussels native, Magali originally comes from the corporate world. After three master’s degrees and several years in her career, she decided to change course completely to follow what she is truly passionate about: Brussels, Belgium, their history and their incredible way of life.

She became a qualified tour guide and founded Beer My Guest in 2015 with a very simple idea: to show people her city the way she herself would like to discover it.

No cookie-cutter routes, no addresses chosen just because they’re touristy. The idea is rather to open the right doors, tell the right stories and introduce you to Belgium through the people who bring it to life.

Over the years, Belgian beer has become one of her great specialities. A qualified brewer and beer expert, Magali guides both the simply curious and true enthusiasts looking for a more in-depth experience.

But today Beer My Guest goes far beyond beer: gastronomy, chocolate, heritage, art, architecture, cycling, games, unusual venues, events… Belgium is our playground.

A local, passionate… and carefully chosen team

At Beer My Guest, we don’t work with just anyone.

Our guides and hosts are selected as much for their personality and their ability to share their knowledge as for the knowledge itself.

Because you can know Brussels inside out and still be boring. And that’s not really what we’re about.

We want local, passionate, dynamic and professional people who can adapt to their group and, above all, make sure everyone has a great time.

We work in French, Dutch and English and can organise experiences in many other languages as needed.

Why Beer My Guest?

Because Belgian beer is part of our story… but certainly not the whole story!

Beer is one of our specialities and a wonderful gateway to Belgian culture. But behind the name Beer My Guest lies, above all, an invitation: come and discover Belgium with us.

Chocolate, gastronomy, heritage, architecture, art, unusual places, local stories or, of course, Belgian beer… we love our country in all its facets and, above all, we love sharing it.

Beer My Guest is much more than beer. It’s an invitation to experience Belgium from the inside.

Our values:

Curious by nature.
