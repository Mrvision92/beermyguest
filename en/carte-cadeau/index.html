Gift card

Looking for an original gift? Our gift cards are ideal! The card is valid on all products on the website: the Brussels treasure hunts, the beer day, one of our monthly beer tastings listed in the calendar… Pure joy!

★★★★★ 553+ reviews

How do I give a gift card?

All gift cards bought on the website are valid across the whole site: the treasure hunt, the beer day, the tastings in the calendar, or any other private group tasting.

1 / Choose the amount of the gift card

2 / You can add a personal message: simply include it in the comments when confirming your purchase, or send it to me at magali@beermyguest.be

3 / You will receive the digital gift card with the discount code and instructions

4 / The gift card is valid for 1 year from the date of purchase

How do I use the gift card?

All gift cards bought on the website are valid across the whole site and for all activities.

1 / Have your discount code to hand

2 / Choose the activity that suits you, “treasure hunt” or “beer day”.

3 / Before paying, enter the code in the discount code field and apply it. You will then see the gift card amount deducted from the total price.

4 / For a private tasting or tailor-made activity, please email us the gift voucher at magali@beermyguest.be.

Don’t hesitate to get in touch with any questions, we’re all ears!
